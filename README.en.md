# CSDATA · CS2 Market Monitoring

Observe item-market changes, rule-based signals, and public Steam inventories in one dashboard.

CSDATA is a web-based tool for players and market observers interested in Counter-Strike 2 items. It brings together changes in prices, buy orders, sale listings, and public inventories, helping you move from a market overview to the individual items and records worth a closer look.

**[Visit CSDATA](https://cs2monitor.cn)**

**Website language:** The website interface is currently primarily in Chinese. This English introduction explains what the service offers and how to get started.

> This repository contains product information and usage guidance. CSDATA is provided as an online service; its application source code is not published here.

## What you can explore

| What you want to understand | What to look at in CSDATA |
| --- | --- |
| When has market activity changed, and which item categories are involved? | Activity trends and category rankings on the overview page |
| What changed in an item's buy orders, sale listings, or lowest listed price? | Item activity records and historical changes |
| Which items have triggered conditions worth checking? | Rule-based signals and signal history |
| What changed in the public inventories being monitored? | Inventory snapshots, change records, and activity rankings |
| How can I follow a particular public inventory? | Custom inventory monitoring, subject to account access and limits |

Available features, history ranges, and usage limits depend on the current service offering and your account's entitlements.

## From an overview to the details

**Start with trends.** Look at changes in buy orders and sale listings, then use category rankings to find where activity is concentrated.

**Read the underlying records.** Open the relevant item, signal, or inventory page. Check the date, source platform, direction of change, and recorded values together.

**Verify the current situation.** Cross-check prices, listings, and inventory information on the relevant platform before making your own decisions.

The website offers desktop and mobile layouts, light and dark themes, and a help center with explanations of its indicators. The page's last successful load time is separate from the collection time of each underlying record.

## Public inventory monitoring

The website uses the term “大商” for accounts included in its public Steam inventory monitoring. These accounts are a selected monitoring set, not a complete view of all market participants or a certification of anyone's identity.

Inventory pages show the latest successfully collected snapshot. Change records compare successive valid snapshots. Privacy settings, access restrictions, or collection failures can leave gaps in the records.

Custom monitoring targets are visible to the account owner and site administrators. They are not automatically added to the public monitoring list or public broadcasts. Availability, target limits, and duration depend on your account's access.

## Getting started

1. Open **[cs2monitor.cn](https://cs2monitor.cn)** and follow the on-screen registration or sign-in instructions.
2. Start with the overview, then open an item or inventory module that interests you.
3. Use the help center for indicator definitions and your account area for current access and plan details.

Basic access includes the homepage overview and selected previews. Full historical records, detailed signals, monitored-account information, and custom monitoring depend on account entitlements. Check the website for current prices, limits, and availability.

## How to interpret the data

- **Listing changes are not completed trades.** Changes in buy-order or sale-listing counts reflect differences between valid observations, not a verified transaction count.
- **Inventory changes do not prove purchases or sales.** Public snapshots alone cannot establish why an item entered or left an inventory.
- **Signals are prompts for further checking.** A rule match does not confirm a participant's intent or predict a future price with certainty.

Data can be delayed, missing, or inconsistent across platforms. CSDATA provides information for market observation, not trade execution, investment advice, or a guarantee of returns. Use of the service is subject to the current user agreement, privacy policy, and disclaimer shown on the website.
