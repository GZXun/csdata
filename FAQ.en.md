# Frequently Asked Questions

[Back to introduction](README.en.md) · [Feature guide](FEATURES.en.md) · [Visit CSDATA](https://cs2monitor.cn)

## Is this an open-source project?

This GitHub repository is CSDATA's public introduction. It contains product documentation and presentation assets. The website's application source code is not published here, and no open-source software license is provided here.

Files in this public repository can be viewed, downloaded, or forked using GitHub. Publishing an introduction does not publish the website's source code. See the [content and rights notice](NOTICE.en.md) for details.

## Do I need to download or install anything?

To use CSDATA, visit [cs2monitor.cn](https://cs2monitor.cn) and follow the registration or sign-in instructions. Downloading this repository does not provide a website application you can deploy yourself.

## Is the website available in English?

The website interface is currently primarily in Chinese. An English-language version of the website is planned for a future update. A release date has not been announced.

In the meantime, the [English introduction](README.en.md), [feature guide](FEATURES.en.md), this FAQ, and the [content and rights notice](NOTICE.en.md) explain the service in English.

## Is CSDATA an item-trading platform?

CSDATA organizes data for market observation and inventory monitoring. It does not match item trades, hold customer funds, trade on users' behalf, or guarantee returns.

## Are all features free?

Basic access includes the homepage overview and selected previews. Full history, detailed signals, monitored-account information, and custom monitoring are available according to account entitlements. Check the website's current plan descriptions for prices, duration, limits, and available content.

## Is the data real-time?

Each module updates according to its collection schedule. Third-party interfaces, network conditions, inventory privacy settings, and access restrictions may affect updates. Different modules may update at different times; second-by-second synchronization is not guaranteed.

The page's last successful load time tells you when that page request succeeded. It does not mean every underlying record was collected at that time.

## Does a decrease in buy orders or sale listings mean a trade took place?

Not necessarily. Activity records show differences in quantities between successive valid observations. Cancellations, relisting, and other factors can change those quantities. They are not records of confirmed trades.

## Do inventory additions or removals prove a purchase or sale?

No. Public inventory snapshots show changes in visible items. By themselves, they cannot confirm the reason for a change, whether a trade occurred, or who controls an account. Privacy settings or collection failures can also affect inventory pages.

## Should I trade based on a signal alone?

A signal only indicates that predefined conditions have been met. Check current prices, listing counts, historical changes, liquidity, and other sources. Do not treat signals as promises of returns or automated trading instructions.

## Are my custom monitoring targets made public?

Custom monitoring targets are private to your account and are not automatically added to the public monitoring list or public broadcasts. Target limits and access duration depend on your current account entitlements.

## Can I use CSDATA on my phone?

Yes. The website provides a mobile layout and can be opened in a browser. Light and dark themes are also supported.

## How can I report a problem?

Contact [support@cs2monitor.cn](mailto:support@cs2monitor.cn) with the affected page, approximate time, steps taken, expected result, and actual result. Handle account-related issues through private support channels rather than public comments.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, redemption codes, or session credentials. Redact your email address and other personal information from screenshots.
